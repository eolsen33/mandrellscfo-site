Controller services
& audits.

Dedicated controller oversight, with periodic internal reviews against industry best practices.

How it works

A controller reviews the work a clerk enters, which gives you multiple levels of oversight and real segregation of duties instead of concentrating financial access in one or two employees.

Each quarter includes a controls and risk check. Where a review finds a gap, we recommend the control changes that reduce errors, leakage and fraud exposure.

Who it's for

We work with owner-operated, service-based businesses from start-up to $30M in revenue, particularly in the trades, where margins live in the details. Our team of ten works from 235 South Main Street in Gainesville, Florida, and serves clients across the state.

What's included

  • Dedicated controller oversight
  • Internal reviews of policies, procedures and systems
  • Approval processes and spending controls
  • Segregation of duties
  • Documentation standards
  • Control changes that mitigate loss and reduce exposure
